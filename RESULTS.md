## Night → Morning signal

- 1,820 / 2,188 = **83.18%** of Night-created Tier-1 tickets received their first response during Morning.
- 1,945 / 2,188 = **88.89%** of Night-created Tier-1 tickets were ultimately resolved by a Morning-shift agent.
- 35.42% of Morning-attributed Tier-1 tickets originated during Night.
- Counterfactual benchmark: moving Night-created Tier-1 tickets from the observed 65.72% breach rate to the observed 9.27% non-night rate corresponds to approximately **1,235 fewer breaches in the supplied sample**. This is a scenario, not a forecast.

## Important interpretation

The evidence supports prioritising overnight queue/handoff investigation. It does not establish individual-agent causation because the export does not contain complete queue/event history.
